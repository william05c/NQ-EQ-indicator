Range indicator — what was measured, on what, and why

generated 2026-10-22T15:22:38.927Z · 765 sessions · 102 band touches · $10000 per trade · 2 bp round-trip cost

Three separate things - do not mix them up

1. The daily model. predicts the session high/low band from prior daily bars. Measured by MAE and band coverage (see docs/02-method). It is a trading strategy.

2. The intraday nowcast. given the running high/low, P(new high) and P(new low). Measured by calibration below. It is a trading strategy.

3. A trading rule. entry at a band touch, exit by target/stop/close. Measured by win rate, expectancy, t-stat. It is the indicator.

Every win rate on this page belongs to object 3. It is a property of a rule someone wrote, using the indicator's levels as entry prices. The indicator itself has no win rate.

Definitions used

H = H = half the band width = (upper - lower band)/2. Target and stop are quoted as multiples of H so sessions of different volatility are comparable.

Win = net P&L per trade > 0 after the round-trip cost. A 90% win rate with a large average loss can still lose money; that is why expectancy and the stop count are shown beside every win rate.

Hours traded = the window in which the entry may occur. The full session runs 18:00–17:00 ET; the cash window 09:30–16:00 ET.

Alignment = daily series is back-adjusted; bands rescaled per session by intradayOpen/dailyOpen.
